  Impact Sounds (1.0, 2019)  impact*, footstep_grass_*, footstep_concrete_*, footstep_wood_*
  RPG Audio                  footstep00-05, knifeSlice*, chop, cloth*, handleCoins*, doorOpen_*,
                             metalLatch, metalClick, beltHandle*, clothBelt*
  Interface Sounds (1.0, 2020)  click_*, select_*, open_*, close_*, error_*

Converted for the game: mixed down to mono, trailing silence trimmed, footsteps shortened with a
fade-out, and re-encoded as Ogg Opus. Credit is not required by the licence; it is given with thanks.

The .m4a files are the same clips re-encoded as AAC for iPhone and iPad, whose browsers may not play Ogg.
